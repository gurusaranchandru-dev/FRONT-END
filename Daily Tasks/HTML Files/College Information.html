Selvamm College of Arts and Science




College Description

>

Selvamm Arts and Science College (SASC) is a private,
autonomous co-educational institution located in Namakkal,
Tamil Nadu.Established in 2000 by Dr. P. Selvaraj, a pioneer in the poultry industry,
the college operates on a self-supporting basis with a mission to uplift underprivileged
and rural youth through quality higher education. It is affiliated with Periyar University
in Salem, approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC), and accredited with an A+ grade by the NAAC.
